# Related Work Section Scientific Claim-Evidence Audit

This audit evaluates the scientific strength, potential overclaims, and adopted conservative framing for all major assertions in the Related Work section.

| Section | Claim Topic | Evidence Strength | Potential Overclaim | Adopted Conservative Wording |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2.1 Deep Learning for Medical Image Classification | Deep learning achieves radiologist-level performance in some tasks | ESTABLISHED (Literature) | Claiming universal superiority over radiologists across all tasks. | "Deep learning has transformed medical image analysis" with citation to Litjens et al. survey; no universal superiority claim. |
| 2.1 Aggregate Metrics Limitation | Accuracy and AUC may not reveal class-specific failure modes | DEMONSTRATED (Our Study) | Claiming ROC-AUC is invalid or misleading in all contexts. | "These measures capture important aspects of model performance" but "may not reveal critical class-specific failure modes" under skewed prevalence. |
| 2.2 BraTS Benchmark | BraTS provides standardized multi-institutional multi-parametric MRI data | ESTABLISHED (Literature) | Claiming BraTS as the only or best benchmark for glioma analysis. | Cited Menze et al. [9], Bakas et al. [10], and Baid et al. [11] as canonical references without exclusivity claims. |
| 2.3 Class Imbalance Detriment | Imbalanced training distributions detrimentally affect CNN classification | ESTABLISHED (Buda et al.) | Claiming class weighting universally fails or that oversampling is always optimal. | "Oversampling was the most consistently effective mitigation" per Buda et al.; class weighting presented as a "straightforward training-time intervention" without guarantee. |
| 2.4 Focal Loss Calibration Effects | Focal Loss can produce under-confident predictions when gamma is set too high | SUPPORTED (Mukhoti et al.) | Claiming Focal Loss universally causes probability compression across all tasks and datasets. | "Mukhoti et al. observed that Focal Loss can produce under-confident predictions" with "limited systematic attention in the 3D medical imaging classification literature." |
| 2.5 Modern Networks Miscalibrated | Modern deep neural networks are frequently miscalibrated | ESTABLISHED (Guo et al.) | Claiming all neural networks are always miscalibrated. | "Despite achieving high classification accuracy, are frequently miscalibrated, producing overconfident predictions" per Guo et al. |
| 2.6 Temperature Scaling Monotonicity | Temperature scaling preserves ranking and default decision boundary | MATHEMATICAL INVARIANCE | Claiming temperature scaling improves classification performance or solves class imbalance. | "Temperature scaling modifies probability reliability but does not alter discriminative ranking or automatically adjust operational decision thresholds." |
| 2.7 Uncertainty as Clinical Prerequisite | Communicating uncertainty is essential for clinical adoption | SUPPORTED (Kompa et al.) | Claiming uncertainty estimation alone guarantees safe deployment. | "A prerequisite for responsible AI deployment rather than an optional diagnostic supplement." |
| 2.8 Evaluation Gap | Individual dimensions are well-studied but evaluated in isolation | LITERATURE GAP | Claiming nobody has ever studied calibration, uncertainty, or thresholds before. | "These dimensions are predominantly evaluated in isolation"; gap framed as "absence of a controlled, integrated evaluation protocol." |
