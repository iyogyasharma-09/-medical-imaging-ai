# Discussion Section Scientific Claim-Evidence Audit

This audit evaluates the scientific strength, potential overclaims, and recommended conservative wording for all major assertions in the Discussion section.

| Claim Topic | Empirical Evidence | Evidence Strength | Potential Overclaim | Recommended Wording |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 4.1 Decoupling of evaluation dimensions | SwinUNETR Protocol A achieves 0.9330 AUC with 0.0% LGG recall; Focal ResNet18 achieves 0.9541 AUC with 9.75% ECE repaired to 3.41% without changing AUC. | DEMONSTRATED | Claiming that all medical models decouple in this manner universally. | State that discrimination, calibration, class-stratified sensitivity, and decision thresholds frequently decouple under severe class imbalance, as demonstrated on BraTS 2023. |
| 4.2 Insufficiency of ROC-AUC and Accuracy | 93.62% accuracy achieved with 0% minority recall; 0.9330 AUC achieved with majority-only default predictions. | DEMONSTRATED | Claiming ROC-AUC is useless or flawed. | State that ROC-AUC answers a ranking question and accuracy answers an overall sample-weighted question; neither determines the sensitivity achieved at a specific operating threshold. |
| 4.3 Mathematical independence of calibration & ranking | Temperature scaling T*=0.4923 reduced test ECE by 65.0% while test ROC-AUC (0.9541) and tau=0.50 predictions remained strictly invariant. | DEMONSTRATED | Claiming calibration improves classification performance. | State that calibration modifies the statistical reliability of probabilities, but mathematically preserves rank discrimination and default binary classifications. |
| 4.4 Architecture-dependent class weighting response | SwinUNETR improved under Protocol C (0% -> 25% recall), while ResNet18 degraded (75% -> 41.7% recall) and DenseNet121 degraded (66.7% -> 33.3% recall). | DEMONSTRATED (Empirical) | Claiming that class weighting proves architectural inductive bias causally. | State that models exhibited divergent empirical responses to identical weighting protocols, and explicitly state that the underlying causal mechanism was not directly investigated. |
| 4.5 Focal Loss probability compression | Focal ResNet18 had raw ECE=9.75% with T*=0.4923, reducing ECE to 3.41% on locked test cohort. | SUPPORTED (Empirical) | Claiming Focal Loss universally causes probability compression in all datasets. | State that Focal Loss in this experiment was associated with compressed probability outputs, and the fitted temperature below unity indicated that sharpening logits improved validation likelihood and test calibration. |
| 4.6 Architecture reliability profiles | SwinUNETR Protocol A attained 1.12% calibrated ECE while exhibiting 0.0% LGG recall; ResNet18 Protocol C attained 0.99% calibrated ECE with 41.67% recall. | DEMONSTRATED | Declaring CNNs superior to Transformers for medical imaging. | State that reliability is an emergent property from the interaction of data, architecture, loss, and thresholds, and that high calibration quality does not guarantee minority sensitivity. |
| 4.7 Methodological vs Clinical recommendations | Operating points demonstrate continuous trade-offs (e.g. tau=0.35 yielding 66.7% recall with 4 false alarms). | METHODOLOGICAL | Recommending a specific threshold for clinical practice or claiming the model is clinically ready. | Frame as methodological recommendations for clinical AI evaluation pipelines, and emphasize that operational thresholds require prospective validation under pre-specified utility functions. |
